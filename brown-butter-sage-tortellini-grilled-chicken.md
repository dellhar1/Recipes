# Brown Butter Sage Tortellini with Grilled Lemon Chicken

Cheese tortellini with browned mushrooms, spinach, crispy sage, and brown butter, topped with grilled lemon-garlic chicken.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Servings** | 6 |
| **Prep time** | 15 minutes, plus 30 minutes to marinate |
| **Cook time** | 30 minutes |

Adapted from [Cheese Tortellini with Butter, Mushrooms, and Crispy Sage](https://www.saltandlavender.com/cheese-tortellini-with-butter-mushrooms-and-crispy-sage/) by Salt & Lavender.

---

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1.5 lb total)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 1/2 lemon (zest the whole lemon first and save the zest for the sauce)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

### Pasta

- 20 oz refrigerated cheese tortellini
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 20 fresh sage leaves, whole
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 16 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 1 medium shallot, minced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 5 oz baby spinach
- 3/4 cup freshly grated parmesan
- Zest of 1 lemon and juice of the remaining 1/2 lemon
- 1/3 cup toasted walnuts or pine nuts, chopped (optional)

---

## Instructions

### 1. Marinate the chicken

Pound the chicken breasts to an even 3/4-inch thickness. Toss them with the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and pepper. Marinate for at least 30 minutes and no more than 4 hours, because the lemon juice will start to make the texture mealy.

### 2. Grill the chicken

Grill over medium-high heat (about 425 °F) for 5 to 7 minutes per side, until the thickest part reads 165 °F. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, then slice it.

### 3. Boil the tortellini

Cook the tortellini in well-salted water according to the package directions. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water before draining.

### 4. Crisp the sage and brown the butter

Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Fry the sage leaves until crisp, about 1 to 2 minutes, and move them to a paper towel. Keep cooking the butter until it turns golden and smells nutty, about 2 more minutes, then pour it into a small bowl.

### 5. Brown the mushrooms

Raise the heat to medium-high, add the olive oil, and spread the mushrooms in a single layer. Let them sit undisturbed for 3 minutes, then stir and cook until well browned, about 5 more minutes. Add the shallot, garlic, and red pepper flakes, and cook for 1 minute.

### 6. Build the sauce

Add the tortellini, spinach, brown butter, parmesan, lemon zest, remaining lemon juice, and 1/2 cup of the pasta water. Toss until the spinach wilts and the sauce turns glossy, adding more pasta water if it looks dry. Taste before adding any salt.

### 7. Serve

Top each bowl with sliced chicken, crumbled crispy sage, and the toasted nuts.

---

## Notes

- **Sodium:** The tortellini and parmesan already carry a lot of salt, so season the sauce only after tasting it.
- **No grill:** A grill pan or cast-iron skillet works well for the chicken.
- **Mushrooms:** Brown them in a single layer. Crowded mushrooms steam instead of browning and turn rubbery.
- **Heat:** Leave out the red pepper flakes for anyone sensitive to spice.

## What changed from the original

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| Brown butter instead of plain butter | Deeper, nuttier flavor from the same amount of butter |
| Lemon zest and juice | Acid cuts the richness and ties the chicken to the pasta |
| Pasta water | Emulsifies the butter and cheese into a glossy sauce without adding fat |
| Double the mushrooms, plus spinach | More volume and vegetables without sacrificing flavor |
| More garlic and a shallot | One garlic clove is too little for six servings |
| Marinated, pounded grilled chicken | Adds protein; even thickness keeps the chicken from drying out |
