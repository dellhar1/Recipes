Lighter Ginger Broccoli and Beef

A high-protein stir-fry with more beef, more vegetables, and less oil than the original.

  • 4servings
  • 350 kcalper serving
  • 41 gprotein per serving
  • 40 mintotal time

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb lean beef (petite sirloin or eye of round), trimmed and cut into very thin strips
  • 2 tsp coconut oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt, divided
  • 3 medium garlic cloves, minced
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 heads broccoli, cut into florets (about 6 cups)
  • 1 large carrot, thinly sliced
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp ground flax seeds
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper, freshly ground
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth, divided

Preparation

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon juice, ground flax, ginger, black pepper, red pepper flakes, and 1/4 cup of the broth. Set it aside so the flax can thicken the sauce.
  2. Heat 1 tsp of the coconut oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the beef in a single layer, working in two batches if needed, and season with 1/4 tsp of the salt. Sear until browned, about 4 minutes, then remove the beef to a plate and pour off any juice in the pan.
  3. Add the remaining 1 tsp of coconut oil, the garlic, and the mushrooms to the hot pan. Cook until the mushrooms release their liquid and begin to brown, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the broccoli and carrot, season with the remaining 1/4 tsp of salt, pour the sauce over the vegetables, and toss to coat. Cover and cook over medium heat until the broccoli is tender-crisp, about 6 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Return the beef to the pan, add the green onions and the remaining 1/4 cup of broth, and stir until the beef is coated. Simmer until the beef is warmed through, about 2 minutes.

Estimated per serving: about 350 kcal, 41 g protein, and 6 g fiber. A nonstick pan is what makes the reduced oil work.
