Brown Butter Sage Tortellini with Grilled Lemon Chicken

Cheese tortellini with browned mushrooms, spinach, crispy sage, and brown butter, topped with grilled lemon-garlic chicken.

Servings
6
Prep
15 min plus 30 min marinating
Cook
30 min

Adapted from Cheese Tortellini with Butter, Mushrooms, and Crispy Sage by Salt & Lavender.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1.5 lb total)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Pasta

  • 20 oz refrigerated cheese tortellini
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 20 fresh sage leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 16 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 medium shallot, minced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 5 oz baby spinach
  • 3/4 cup freshly grated parmesan
  • Zest of 1 lemon and juice of the remaining 1/2 lemon
  • 1/3 cup toasted walnuts or pine nuts, chopped (optional)

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken

    Pound the chicken breasts to an even 3/4-inch thickness. Zest the lemon and set the zest aside for the sauce. Toss the chicken with the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and pepper. Marinate for at least 30 minutes and no more than 4 hours, because the lemon juice will start to make the texture mealy.

  2. Grill the chicken

    Grill over medium-high heat (about 425 °F) for 5 to 7 minutes per side, until the thickest part reads 165 °F. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, then slice it.

  3. Boil the tortellini

    Cook the tortellini in well-salted water according to the package directions. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water before draining.

  4. Crisp the sage and brown the butter

    Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Fry the sage leaves until crisp, about 1 to 2 minutes, and move them to a paper towel. Keep cooking the butter until it turns golden and smells nutty, about 2 more minutes, then pour it into a small bowl.

  5. Brown the mushrooms

    Raise the heat to medium-high, add the olive oil, and spread the mushrooms in a single layer. Let them sit undisturbed for 3 minutes, then stir and cook until well browned, about 5 more minutes. Add the shallot, garlic, and red pepper flakes, and cook for 1 minute.

  6. Build the sauce

    Add the tortellini, spinach, brown butter, parmesan, lemon zest, remaining lemon juice, and 1/2 cup of the pasta water. Toss until the spinach wilts and the sauce turns glossy, adding more pasta water if it looks dry. Taste before adding any salt.

  7. Serve

    Top each bowl with the sliced chicken, crumbled crispy sage, and toasted nuts.

Notes

  • The tortellini and parmesan already carry a lot of salt, so season the sauce only after tasting it.
  • A grill pan or cast-iron skillet works well for the chicken if you do not want to use the grill.
  • Brown the mushrooms in a single layer. Crowded mushrooms steam instead of browning and turn rubbery.
  • Leave out the red pepper flakes for anyone sensitive to spice.
